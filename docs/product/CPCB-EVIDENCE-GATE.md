# CPCB evidence gate decision

**Decision:** Cut the CPCB conflict scenario from the prototype on 2026-08-27.

The release gate requires two genuine official publications that apply to the
same confirmed Information Need and remain compatible on authority, geography,
period, unit, measure definition, and methodology before a conflict can be
shown. The reviewed CPCB publication index, CPCB introduction/air-monitoring
material, and the 2022–23 annual report describe different publication and
monitoring contexts. They do not, by themselves, establish a material
disagreement for one compatible scope.

The gate records the direct URL, retrieval date, authority, measure, scope,
period, unit, publication-date availability, methodology, and applicability for
each reviewed candidate. It does not infer a publication date where the record
does not state one.

No source bytes, representations, locators, or numbers from these materials
are registered as conflict evidence. The seeded CPCB affordance is disabled,
and CPCB/air-quality free text follows the conservative Outside Snapshot
Coverage path. Issues #19 and #20 must not create an Evidence Conflict until a
future evidence gate records two independently verified compatible sources.
