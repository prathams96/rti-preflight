# Two-minute demo runbook

Use a fresh browser session and a 360px viewport when possible. Keep the citizen journey first; explain architecture only after the result is visible.

## 0:00–1:25 — Citizen journey

1. On Ask, enter: “Between 2021 and 2023 which States reported property stolen up and recovery down?”
2. Choose **Interpret my need**, review the Information Need Card, and choose **Yes, search**.
3. Point out the **Derived Finding** status, the calculated-not-directly-stated disclosure, the 16 matching States/UTs, Gujarat's row, and the inspectable source-cell lineage.
4. Open Search Scope and explain that the prototype checked a frozen Evidence Snapshot; no government system was accessed.
5. Choose **Save/share Evidence Brief** and show the downloadable JSON artifact, or use the browser's share sheet when available.
6. Choose **Still need an official response? Prepare an RTI**, review the editable Filing Draft, and show the verified route and 3,000-character boundary.

## 1:25–2:00 — Trust and boundaries

1. Open Prototype Details: NCRB data is real official public data in a curated snapshot; interpretation/calculation are working; filing, OTP, payment, and acknowledgement are simulated; government integration is absent.
2. Mention that unsupported requests become **Outside Snapshot Coverage**, not “unavailable,” and that the citizen can still prepare a records-focused draft.
3. Switch to Hindi to show that journey state and provenance are preserved.
4. Close with the independent-assistant disclosure: this is not an official RTI response and no request, payment, or personal information was sent to a government system.
