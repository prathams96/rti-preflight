# 1. Builder Brief

## The Challenge

Choose one real problem you have personally faced while using an Indian public-service website or digital service, then build a version of that experience that is simpler, clearer, and more useful.

The problem may relate to areas such as:

- Travel
- Taxes
- Pensions
- Certificates
- Payments
- Grievances
- Any other public need

Services such as IRCTC, EPFO, and the Income Tax portal are examples, not a restricted list of allowed services.

---

## What You Need to Build

The prototype submitted for the hackathon must be:

- Built using **Codex**, or
- Powered by an **OpenAI model**

Codex must play a meaningful role in the actual development process. It should not be added only for the purpose of the submission.

If a project reaches the **10-finalist stage**, OpenAI will provide support.

The prototype should solve the chosen problem through a **complete citizen journey**.

Where production access would be unsafe, restricted, or unavailable, builders should use:

- Mock data
- Mock accounts
- Simulated backend behavior
- Simulated integrations

Reviewers will evaluate the **citizen-facing experience**, not an admin dashboard.

### The prototype should:

- Solve one clearly defined user problem.
- Allow the reviewer to complete the main journey from beginning to end.
- Be easier to understand or use than the current experience.
- Be designed for real Indian users.
- Work thoughtfully for mobile users, slower internet connections, and people with limited digital experience.
- Use mock or synthetic data whenever the real flow would involve personal information, payments, OTPs, or government systems.

---

## What a Strong Build Looks Like

A strong submission should make the following clear:

1. **Who has the problem?**
2. **What is difficult about the current experience?**
3. **What has your solution changed?**
4. **Why is your approach better?**
5. **Which parts work today and which parts are mocked?**
6. **How could the idea operate safely at larger scale?**

The complete citizen journey should be demonstrated.

A static design is not sufficient. The user interface and interactions should work from start to finish.

Mocked data, dependencies, and integrations should be clearly disclosed.

---

## What Not to Do

Do not:

- Access, test, or interfere with a live government system.
- Reverse-engineer private systems.
- Use undocumented private APIs.
- Scrape personal or restricted information.
- Use real Aadhaar numbers.
- Use real PAN details.
- Use real passwords.
- Use real OTPs.
- Use real payment details.
- Use real health information.
- Use other sensitive personal data.
- Present the prototype as an official government product.
- Use government logos in a way that suggests an official partnership, endorsement, or approval.
- Submit an existing old project with only minor modifications.
- Include code, assets, or data that you do not have permission to use.

## What to Submit

### Submission deadline

**August 28, 2026 at 8:00 PM IST**

There is **no grace period** after the form closes.

### Required submission materials

#### 1. Live public prototype

Provide a browser-accessible public link.

It must:

- Open without requesting reviewer access.
- Work directly in a browser.
- Not require reviewers to download a mobile app.

#### 2. Demo video

Submit one video of **no more than two minutes**.

- **Minute 2:** Explain how you built it and why you made the key product and technical choices.

#### 3. Project summary

Provide a summary of **under 250 words** explaining:

- What the project is.
- What problem it solves.
- Why it is better than the current solution.

#### 4. Teammate details

If you are participating as a team of two:

- Both teammates must register.
- Each teammate must submit the other person's **registered email address**.

If you are participating alone, leave the partner-email field blank.

### Final submission check

Every submitted link should work without requesting additional access.

Yes.

- In a team of two

## Scope of the Build

### What exactly should I build?

Choose a specific problem within a public-service website or digital journey and create a better solution.

You may either:

- Improve one focused part of an existing experience, or
- Rethink the broader end-to-end experience.

---

### Is a Figma design enough?

No.

Figma designs can be included as part of the project, but the primary citizen journey must be a **working prototype**.

---

### Does every feature need to work?

Every feature that you **demo** must work.

If you show or discuss a feature during the finalist presentation, reviewers should be able to see it functioning rather than relying on an explanation of what it would eventually do.

---

## Tools and Rules

### Is Codex mandatory?

Yes, for the prototype submitted to this hackathon.

Codex must make a meaningful contribution to building the project.

You may use other:

- Development tools
- Frameworks
- Libraries
- Supporting technologies

Your submission should explain how Codex contributed.

If a public-service organization later decides to develop the solution further, the hackathon's Codex requirement does not restrict the technology used for that future implementation. The builder and public-service body may choose the technology best suited to the project.

If the build reaches the **10-finalist stage**, OpenAI will provide support.

---

### Can I use other tools or libraries?

Yes.

You must:

- Have the right to use them.
- Disclose their use in the submission.

---

### Can I use an existing government website as a starting point?

You may study an existing government website to understand its user problems.

However, you may not:

- Copy its source code.
- Build directly on private infrastructure.
- Reverse-engineer private systems.

The submitted prototype should be an **independent build**.

---

### Can I connect to a live government system?

No, unless the organizers explicitly provide an approved public sandbox.

Otherwise, use:

- Mock data
- Simulated integrations
- Simulated backend behavior

---

### Can I use real user data?

No.

Use fictional or synthetic data.

Do not enter real:

- Identification numbers
- Passwords
- OTPs
- Payment information
- Sensitive personal information

---

### Can I use official logos?

Do not use a government or official logo in a manner that makes the prototype look official, approved, or endorsed.

The prototype should be clearly identified as an **independent hackathon prototype**.

---

### Can I submit something I already built?

The submission should be created for this hackathon.

You may use standard:

- Starter templates

provided their licences allow it.
